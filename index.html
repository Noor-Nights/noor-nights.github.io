اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ
Allāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar, Lā ilāha illallāh, wallāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar, wa lillāhil-ḥamd
Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah. And Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. And all praise is due to Allah
Virtue: Recite abundantly in the Ten Days of Dhul Hijjah — Ibn ʿUmar and Abu Hurayrah would proclaim it aloud in the markets. — Bukhārī (muʿallaq)
الفضل: أكثِر منه في أيام ذي الحجة العشرة — كان ابن عمر وأبو هريرة رضي الله عنهما يجهران به في الأسواق. — البخاري (معلقاً)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ
SubḥānAllāhi wa biḥamdihi, SubḥānAllāhil-ʿAẓīm
Glory be to Allah and all praise is due to Him; Glory be to Allah, the Magnificent
Virtue: "Two words light on the tongue, heavy on the Scale, beloved to the Most Merciful." — Bukhārī & Muslim
الفضل: "كلمتان خفيفتان على اللسان، ثقيلتان في الميزان، حبيبتان إلى الرحمن." — البخاري ومسلم
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr
None has the right to be worshipped except Allah alone, no partner has He; His is the dominion and His is the praise and He is over all things All-Powerful
Virtue: Equivalent to freeing ten slaves, 100 good deeds written, 100 sins erased, protection from Shaytān all day. — Bukhārī
الفضل: تعدل عتق عشر رقاب، وتُكتب له مئة حسنة، وتُمحى عنه مئة سيئة، وتكون حرزاً من الشيطان يومه. — البخاري
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ
SubḥānAllāhi wa biḥamdihi
Glory be to Allah and all praise is due to Him
Virtue: Sins forgiven even if they are as much as the foam of the sea. — Muslim
الفضل: حُطَّت خطاياه وإن كانت مثل زبد البحر. — مسلم
اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ
Allāhumma aʿinnī ʿalā dhikrika wa shukrika wa ḥusni ʿibādatik
O Allah, help me to remember You, to be grateful to You, and to worship You in the best manner
Virtue: The Prophet ﷺ commanded Muʿādh ibn Jabal to say this after every prayer. — Abū Dāwūd (Ṣaḥīḥ)
الفضل: أوصى به النبي ﷺ معاذ بن جبل أن يقوله دُبُر كل صلاة. — أبو داود (صحيح)
حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ، عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ
Ḥasbiyallāhu lā ilāha illā huwa, ʿalayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa rabbul-ʿarshil-ʿaẓīm
Allah is sufficient for me; there is no deity except Him. I have relied upon Him, and He is Lord of the Magnificent Throne
Virtue: Allah will suffice him in whatever worries him of this world and the Hereafter. — Abū Dāwūd (Ṣaḥīḥ)
الفضل: كفاه الله ما أهمه من أمر الدنيا والآخرة. — أبو داود (صحيح)
أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
Aṣbaḥnā wa aṣbaḥal-mulku lillāh, wal-ḥamdu lillāh, lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr
We have reached the morning and at this very time the dominion belongs to Allah. All praise is for Allah. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, alone, without partner
Virtue: From the morning adhkar of the Prophet ﷺ. — Abū Dāwūd
الفضل: من أذكار الصباح النبوية. — أبو داود
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ
Allāhumma bika aṣbaḥnā, wa bika amsaynā, wa bika naḥyā, wa bika namūtu, wa ilaykan-nushūr
O Allah, by Your leave we have reached the morning, and by Your leave we have reached the evening, by Your leave we live and die, and unto You is our resurrection
Virtue: Morning adhkar of the Prophet ﷺ. — Tirmidhī (Ḥasan)
الفضل: من أذكار الصباح النبوية. — الترمذي (حسن)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Bismillāhil-ladhī lā yaḍurru maʿasmihi shay'un fil-arḍi wa lā fis-samā'i wa huwas-samīʿul-ʿalīm
In the name of Allah, with Whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, All-Knowing
Virtue: Nothing will harm him. — Abū Dāwūd & Tirmidhī (Ṣaḥīḥ)
الفضل: لم يضره شيء. — أبو داود والترمذي (صحيح)
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
Aʿūdhu bikalimātillāhit-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq
I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of what He has created
Virtue: No harm from anything will afflict him until he moves on from that place. — Muslim
الفضل: لم يضره شيء حتى يرتحل من مكانه ذلك. — مسلم
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَصْبَحْتُ أُشْهِدُكَ، وَأُشْهِدُ حَمَلَةَ عَرْشِكَ، وَمَلَائِكَتَكَ، وَجَمِيعَ خَلْقِكَ، أَنَّكَ أَنْتَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ، وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُكَ وَرَسُولُكَ
Allāhumma innī aṣbaḥtu ushhiduka, wa ushhidu ḥamalata ʿarshika, wa malā'ikataka, wa jamīʿa khalqika, annaka antallāhu lā ilāha illā anta waḥdaka lā sharīka lak, wa anna Muḥammadan ʿabduka wa rasūluk
O Allah, I have risen to bear witness — with the bearers of Your Throne, Your angels, and all creation — that You are Allah, none has the right to be worshipped except You alone, no partner have You, and that Muḥammad is Your servant and Messenger
Virtue: A quarter of himself will be freed from the Fire. — Abū Dāwūd (Ḥasan)
الفضل: أعتق الله ربعه من النار. — أبو داود (حسن)

🌟 About Noor Nights

The first ten days of Dhul Hijjah are among the most blessed days of the year. Allah swore by them in the Quran, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "There are no days on which righteous deeds are more beloved to Allah than these ten days." (Bukhari)

This app helps you track your daily worship goals, fast and make du'a on Arafah Day, access comprehensive prophetic supplications (Jawami' ad-Du'a), and stay spiritually engaged throughout these ten precious days.

Please keep the developer and all those involved in your sincere prayers.

Get the App

Install Noor Nights directly on your device for the best experience and reliable background notifications.

1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari. ⎙

2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen ➕.

⚠️ Safari is required. Notifications only work after adding to Home Screen.
