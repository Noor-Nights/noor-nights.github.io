🌟 About Noor Nights

The first ten days of Dhul Hijjah are among the most blessed days of the year. Allah swore by them in the Quran, and the Prophet ﷺ said: "There are no days on which righteous deeds are more beloved to Allah than these ten days." (Bukhari)

This app helps you track your daily worship goals, fast and make du'a on Arafah Day, access comprehensive prophetic supplications (Jawami' ad-Du'a), and stay spiritually engaged throughout these ten precious days.

Please keep the developer and all those involved in your sincere prayers.
